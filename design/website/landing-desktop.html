03 / The halving steps

Half the coins.
Twice the price.

Of the coins on the curve, era 1 sells 8/15, era 2 sells 4/15, era 3 sells 2/15 and era 4 sells 1/15. Each era raises the same HYPE, so the average price doubles at every step. Graduation moves the pool to Meteora with the liquidity locked.

  • Era 18/15 of the coins · 1× price
  • Era 24/15 of the coins · 2× price
  • Era 32/15 of the coins · 4× price
  • Era 41/15 of the coins · 8× price
